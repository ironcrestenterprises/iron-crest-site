Privacy

This site is run by Iron Crest Enterprises LLC, Los Angeles, California. This policy explains what we collect through it and what we do with it.

What we collect. When you send a message through the contact form, we receive the name, company, email address and message you enter. Like most websites, our host also keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and performance. We do not use those logs to identify you.

How we use it. We use your details only to answer your inquiry and for any business that follows from it. We do not sell them, rent them or add you to a mailing list.

Who else receives it. Form messages are delivered to us through our website host, Netlify. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address so it can send them to your browser. We do not share your details with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking. This site does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics. No other company collects information about your activity across websites through this site.

Do Not Track. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the site works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Reviewing or removing your details. To see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, email info@ironcrestenterprises.com.

Changes to this policy. If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date below.
